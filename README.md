# Laser Clinics UK Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Laser Clinics UK is a multi-location medical aesthetics and beauty clinic network offering laser hair removal, skin treatments and cosmetic injectables. With more than 40 clinics across the UK, the brand provides professional aesthetic treatments through specialist therapists, registered nurses and medical practitioners.

The redesigned homepage focuses on clearer brand messaging, streamlined treatment discovery and easier access to local clinic information, creating a more structured customer journey.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three key homepage and user-experience problems.

### 1. Clearer First Impression and Value Proposition
- Introduced a stronger hero section with a clear headline and supporting message.
- Made the brand's main treatment services easier to understand.
- Added two prominent actions: explore treatments and find a clinic.
- Created a clearer starting point for visitors navigating the website.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery
- Organised the treatment offering into six clearly defined service categories.
- Introduced dedicated icons and short descriptions for each category.
- Provided direct links to relevant treatment information.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify and explore available services.

### 3. More Prominent Clinic Discovery
- Introduced a dedicated clinic-finder section on the homepage.
- Increased the visibility of clinic-finding links within the navigation.
- Added prominent mobile actions to improve access to local clinic information.
- Gave greater visibility to the brand's nationwide clinic network.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Strengthened the opening section to communicate the brand's services and positioning more effectively.
- **Layout:** Created a clearer hierarchy between the hero, treatment categories and clinic-finder sections.
- **User experience:** Simplified the journey from discovering treatments to finding a suitable clinic.
- **Navigation:** Improved access to treatment information and clinic locations through more prominent links.
- **Mobile experience:** Prioritised clinic discovery through more visible mobile actions.
- **Overall appearance:** Established a more structured, service-focused homepage that supports the needs of visitors exploring aesthetic treatments.

## 4. Project Note

The three identified improvements are strategic homepage and user-experience recommendations implemented in the redesign. They represent design observations rather than verified technical faults or findings from a formal usability study.
